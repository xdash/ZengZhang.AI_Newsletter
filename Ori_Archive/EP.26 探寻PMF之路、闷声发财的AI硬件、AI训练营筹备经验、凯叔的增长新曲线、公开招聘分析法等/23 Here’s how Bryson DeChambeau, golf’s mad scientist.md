# Here’s how Bryson DeChambeau, golf’s mad scientist, is using AI as a swing coach

> 原链: https://www.fastcompany.com/91410129/how-bryson-dechambeau-is-using-ai-to-fix-his-golf-swing  
> 来源: www.fastcompany.com · 归档自 EP.26 · 抓取于 2026-08-06  

---

Artificial intelligence is infiltrating every corner of professional sports, from [scouting](https://www.fastcompany.com/91270187/sports-most-innovative-companies-2025) and injury prevention to scheduling. Now, it looks like golf has its most sophisticated [AI](https://www.fastcompany.com/section/artificial-intelligence "AI") adoption yet, and it’s happening in the bag of Bryson DeChambeau, the sport’s [most notorious tinkerer](https://www.fastcompany.com/91140086/bryson-dechambeaus-3d-printed-irons-may-be-the-future-of-golf). 

“We’re building an AI golf coach,” DeChambeau says. “Essentially, it will be a golf coach that, based on data, will be able to tell you exactly what you’re doing, how to practice, and how to improve your game. We can take a golf swing, compile the information, upload it, and within a minute, it will give me what’s different from my gold standard set of swings.”
