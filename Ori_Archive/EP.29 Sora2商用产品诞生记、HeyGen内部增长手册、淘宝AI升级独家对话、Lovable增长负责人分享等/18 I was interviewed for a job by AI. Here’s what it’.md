# I was interviewed for a job by AI. Here’s what it’s like

> 原链: https://www.fastcompany.com/91422082/i-was-interviewed-by-ai  
> 来源: www.fastcompany.com · 归档自 EP.29 · 抓取于 2026-08-06  

---

I was interviewing for a job as a customer service agent with Anna. She had a low, pleasant voice and she’d nailed the pronunciation of my name—something few people do. I wanted to make a good impression except I had no idea what Anna was thinking because Anna couldn’t think. Anna wasn’t technically a person. She was [AI](https://www.fastcompany.com/section/artificial-intelligence "AI").

Not only is AI changing how we do our jobs, it’s also [changing how we _get_ jobs](https://www.fastcompany.com/91266889/4-ways-ai-is-changing-how-you-get-a-job). This ranges from [using AI to screen resumes, schedule interviews](https://www.fastcompany.com/91246983/job-search-2025-3-key-ways-ai-will-make-an-impact-on-recruitment), even conduct them. According to a 2025 report, [20% of companies are using AI to interview candidates](https://www.testgorilla.com/skills-based-hiring/state-of-skills-based-hiring-2025/#sbhr-section-container-0).

Even so, nothing can replace human recruiters, the folks who’ve deployed Anna into the wild stressed to me. After I spoke with her, I quickly understood why.
